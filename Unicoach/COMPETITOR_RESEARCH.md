## 🔑 5. Keyword Strategy & Opportunities

By looking at LeapScholar's non-branded search terms, we can see exactly what students are searching for when they find the site:

### A. High-Value Organic Search Terms (No Ad Spend)
These keywords represent topics where UniCoach should write high-quality blog posts and articles immediately to capture free search traffic:
1. **"abroad scholarship for indian students after 12th"** (High intent, students looking for funding).
2. **"royal central school of speech and drama acceptance rate"** (Specific college inquiries).
3. **"harvard university" / "harward university"** (Typo traffic).
4. **"kcl"** (King's College London).

### B. High-Intent Paid Search Terms (Competitor is paying for these)
LeapScholar runs advertisements on these high-converting search keywords. We can build landing pages targeting these terms:
1. **"imperial college london"**
2. **"national university of singapore"**
3. **"sat practice test" / "sat"**
4. **"ielts mock test"**

## 🎯 6. Strategic SEO Action Plan for UniCoach

To compete effectively, the UniCoach platform should implement these strategies:

1. **Build Free Resource Hubs:**
   * Create an **"IELTS Mock Test & Prep"** section and a **"SAT Practice Test"** landing page (highly searched terms).
2. **Scholarship-Focused Blogs:**
   * Write dedicated guides on *"Undergraduate Scholarships for Indian Students"* and *"Masters Funding in UK/USA"*.
3. **Target High-Referral Outgoing Destinations:**
   * LeapScholar redirects significant traffic to **englishtest.duolingo.com** (Duolingo English Test) and **leapfinance.com**. UniCoach should emphasize Duolingo English Test guides and partner with study abroad loan providers.
