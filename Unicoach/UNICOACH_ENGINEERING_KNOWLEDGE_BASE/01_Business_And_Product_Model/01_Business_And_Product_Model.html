1. Lead Management System (Custom CRM vs Salesforce)Product Strategy
What is it?
A custom-built CRM pipeline in the Admin Portal that tracks every student lead from initial form submission through university enrollment.
How does it work & Problem solved?
Instead of losing student inquiries in WhatsApp chats or spreadsheets, every form submission creates a tracked Lead document in MongoDB. Admins drag-and-drop across Kanban stages, AI auto-scores leads on eligibility, and automatic WhatsApp notifications keep students informed.
What alternatives existed?
Salesforce CRM: $150/user/month — prohibitive for an early-stage startup. Google Sheets: No automation, no status tracking, no AI scoring.
Why did we choose this approach?
Full domain ownership: custom lead statuses, AI scoring logic, direct WhatsApp integration, and zero SaaS dependency cost.
2. B2C vs B2B Revenue Model DecisionBusiness Model
What is it?
UniCoach operates a B2C model — charging students directly for consultancy services — rather than a B2B model targeting universities for enrollment commissions.
How does it work & Problem solved?
Students pay service fees (INR 15,000–80,000) for end-to-end university application support. Revenue is recognized at enrollment confirmation, not at lead acquisition.
What alternatives existed?
B2B University Commission Model: Longer payment cycles (6–18 months), dependent on foreign university partnerships, subject to regulatory compliance across multiple countries.
Why did we choose this approach?
Faster cash conversion cycle, full control over pricing, and direct student relationship ownership.
