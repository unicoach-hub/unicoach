1. Groq LPU + Llama-3 vs OpenAI GPT-4LLM Infrastructure
What is it?
Groq's Language Processing Unit (LPU) is custom AI inference silicon that delivers 750 tokens/second on open-source models like Meta's Llama-3 8B.
How does it work & Problem solved?
GPU-based inference (OpenAI, Anthropic) is bottlenecked by memory bandwidth — the model weights are read from VRAM thousands of times per token. Groq's LPU uses streaming linear algebra that eliminates this bottleneck, delivering 12x higher throughput and sub-450ms TTFT.
What alternatives existed?
OpenAI GPT-4o: Higher quality reasoning, 1.5–4s TTFT, $0.005–$0.015/query. Anthropic Claude: Similar pricing and latency profile to OpenAI.
Why did we choose this approach?
At 750 tokens/second and $0.001/query, Groq + Llama-3 delivers a ChatGPT-like streaming UX at 10x lower cost — critical for a startup where AI chat is a core product feature, not an occasional premium add-on.
2. AI Lead Scoring System (0–100 Eligibility Score)AI Feature
What is it?
An automated lead qualification system that uses Llama-3 to score every new student lead from 0–100 based on their GPA, IELTS score, budget, and target destination.
How does it work & Problem solved?
Without AI scoring, admins spend 5–10 minutes per lead manually reviewing eligibility. The scoring system runs automatically on lead creation, generating a structured JSON response with a numeric score and reasoning. Admins focus first on leads scoring ≥70.
What alternatives existed?
Manual scoring rubric: Inconsistent, subjective, time-consuming. Rule-based formula: Cannot handle nuanced cases (e.g., low GPA but high IELTS for a practical program).
Why did we choose this approach?
Eliminates 80% of admin time spent on lead triage. LLM reasoning handles nuanced eligibility edge cases that simple rule formulas cannot capture.
